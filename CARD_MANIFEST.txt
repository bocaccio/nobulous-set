THE NOBULOUS SET — CARD FILES  (rev 14)
Generated from Compendium v22 / AoS v10 / NAP v0.9c / BAT v0.6 / NRJ v0.24

CHANGES IN THIS REVISION
  - NAP Tasks: full-width green REQUEST band across the top of the middle
    section, full-width red DEMAND band (rotated 180) across the bottom.
  - NAP Task titles wrap to two rows on the left spine, clear of the corner.
  - Top-left corner stack, top to bottom: subject icon, recovery-colour dot,
    skulls.  Subject icons: Anatomy (heart, maroon), Engineering (gear, blue),
    Procedure (scalpel, purple), Theory (book, olive).  Non-Credit Tasks have
    no subject icon.
  - Every NAP deck is now landscape.  Opportunity band gold, Setback band blue,
    Final Exam band black.
  - Morning / Afternoon / Evening set in 30pt over the tinted slot colour.
  - Final Exam cards carry the subject icon beside the recovery dot, matching the
    corner stack on the Credit Tasks.
  - NAP Task cards: Request and Demand text enlarged to 9pt / 23pt numerals, the
    Demand block moved flush to its band, and a hairline rule added at the middle
    to separate the two sides.
  - One die-colour palette across the whole set, on every NAP recovery dot and
    every AoS required-colour dot:
        Yellow #e8b93a   Green #4f9c62   Blue #4a86c8   Purple #8d5bb0   Red #c0392b
  - AoS header bands now follow the source deck colours: Routine #315c49,
    Specialized #4c4a75, Unstable #7a3838, Advanced #8a5a20, Limitation #2f5966,
    Technician #3f5260, Element mat #555555.
  - AoS body text, element numerals and headings enlarged.
  - AoS element allocation mats are portrait with a large empty placement box.
  - Long card titles step down in size so they stay on one line.
  - The Assembly / Tyrant card is removed.  Splices stack beside the Encounter
    deck and become the Tyrant's Upgrades when it awakens; the Tyrant's Health,
    REC and dice pool are in the rulebook.
  - NRJ cards now match their base-game analogue rather than using one NRJ colour:
      Experimental Protocols and Research Notes  BAT green
      Unauthorized Modifications                 BAT category colours + UNAUTHORIZED strip
      Impostor Tasks                             NAP purple
      Following Setbacks                         NAP setback blue
      Devious Opportunities                      NAP opportunity gold
      Nobulous Interference, Backdoor Routines   AoS rust
      Corrupted Limitations                      AoS limitation teal
      Imposter Scenarios                         coloured by domain: Knowledge purple,
                                                 Physical green, Cover-Up rust
    Every NRJ card names its tier in the footer.
  - The four AoS Program decks are now genuinely two-sided: Stable on the face,
    the matching Overheated on the back of the same card.
      TTS  : tick "Unique Backs" in the Custom Deck dialog and load
             <deck>_TTS.png as the face and <deck>_TTS_back.png as the back.
      Print: each deck's PnP pages alternate front page / back page with the
             back columns mirrored, so duplex printing on long-edge flip puts
             Overheated behind its own Stable face.
  - Every NRJ card carries a solid #1f4e6b band along its foot reading NOBULOUS
    RESEARCH JOURNAL.  Face-up, the module cards can be riffled out of any base
    deck in one pass.
  - Impostor Tasks now use the base NAP Task layout: name on the left spine,
    subject icon in the corner, green REQUEST band above and red DEMAND band
    below, and GRANTS NO CREDIT on the right spine where a Task's penalty sits.
  - BAT band colours by function: Encounters, Lab Rats and Assembly keep the
    green house colour; Upgrades and Specimens are coloured by category
    (Power #8a3d2e, Mobility #2c6e63, Defense #3c5a8a, Senses #6b4a8a);
    Specimens add a gold SPECIMEN strip and a warm card tint; Tyrant Attacks
    use #4a1f1f with a cool tint.

TWO-SIDED CARDS
  These decks are genuinely double-sided.  In TTS tick "Unique Backs" and load
  <deck>_TTS.png as the face and <deck>_TTS_back.png as the back.  In print, the
  PnP pages alternate front page / mirrored back page for long-edge duplex.
      BAT_Rats          4 cards   Side A face / Side B back
      NAP_Apprentices   3 cards   two Apprentices per card
      AoS_Technicians   3 cards   two Technicians per card
      AoS_Routine / Specialized / Unstable / Advanced
                        8 cards each   Stable face / Overheated back

  NOBULOUS RESEARCH JOURNAL.

DICE
  Use the 20 Strong Dice and Chip saved object: 4 Purple, 4 Blue, 4 Green,
  4 Yellow, 1 Red, plus the Health, Strategy and Recovery stat dice.

NOTES
  NAP Tasks carry Request and Demand on one landscape card; rotate 180 degrees
  to switch sides.  They are not two-sided cards.
  18 skulls across the 26 base Tasks: 3 cards carry 2, 12 carry 1.
