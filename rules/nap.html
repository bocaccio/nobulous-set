The Nobulous Set · Game Two

Nobulous Apprenticeship
Program (NAP)

A prestigious opportunity. Terms and conditions apply.

One Apprentice, three Time Slots a day, and a workload that does not care how tired you are. Earn Credits across four subjects, survive the things that go wrong, and pass a Final Exam built out of everything you neglected.

Following in Nobulous’ Footsteps (FINF)

FINF is a second dial, independent of the difficulty level, and it works in two directions. The three FINF cards are numbered 1 Morning, 2 Afternoon and 3 Evening. Choose one setting before you start and keep it for the whole game.

Increase — 1 / 2 / 3

Take that many FINF cards at random and shuffle them into the Assignment deck. They share the Assignment back, so they are hidden until drawn. When one is drawn, place it before the Primary selection for that slot and add an Assignment from the top of the deck to the Time Slot the card is numbered for — the Time Slot you are in, or another one — then discard the FINF card. Ignore Skulls on Assignments added by FINF, and never exceed 4 Assignments in a row.

Reduce — 1 / 2 / 3

Take that many FINF cards at random and flip them aside, face‑up. Each is one use. You may remove a non‑Final‑Exam card from the active Time Slot only if you hold the FINF card numbered for that slot: spend it, exhaust every die stored on the removed card, and turn the FINF card face‑down. Holding FINF 2, for example, lets you remove one card during an Afternoon.

FINF is not used during the Final Exam, in either direction.

Difficulty

Difficulty is two numbers: the Credit goal you must reach before the Final Exam, and how many of the 6 Setbacks are shuffled into the Assignment deck. Choose a level before you start and keep it for the whole game.

The Credit goal cannot carry the dial alone: Credits also reduce Final Exam Targets, so asking for more hands some of it back, and past 8 there are not enough Assignments left to earn them. The deck is the other half. The FINF dial is separate again and can be set at any difficulty.

Setup

  1. Choose an Apprentice. All six begin at 2 HP / 2 STR / 2 REC; they differ only in abilities.
  2. Choose a difficulty: Easy 5, Standard 6, Hard 7 or Nobulous 8 Credits.
  3. Shuffle 3 of the 6 Setbacks into the 26 Tasks and 6 Opportunities to form the Assignment deck. Return the other 3 Setbacks to the box.
  4. Choose a FINF setting: Increase 1–3 or Reduce 1–3. Standard is Reduce 1. Take that many of the 3 FINF cards at random; return the rest to the box.
  5. Separate the 12 Final Exams. You will build the exam pool later.
  6. Take all 17 dice as available. Place the Morning Time Slot card as active for Day 1.

Object of the game

Earn your Credit goal — 6 Credits at Standard difficulty — to reach the Final Exam, then survive three exams with at least 1 Health remaining. Credits are earned by completing Credit Tasks and are never spent — completed Tasks remain visible in their Time Slot until End of Day, then enter the face‑up discard pile as the record of that subject.

Theory
How the apprenticeship ends Play Days until either you reach your Credit goal or the Assignment deck runs out. Both send you to the Final Exam; arriving short of the goal just costs you dice (see Leaving the apprenticeship). If your Health ever reaches 0 you lose immediately — during the apprenticeship or during the exams.

The day

Each day has three Time Slots: Morning, Afternoon, Evening. Each slot runs:

  1. Perform the recovery shown on the dial for this slot. (Day 1 Morning has no special recovery.)
  2. Reveal 2 Assignments and take one into the row as your Primary. Discard the other. A Setback among the two must be the one you take. The Primary’s recovery‑color dot sets the color queued for the next Time Slot.
  3. Work Assignments with Strategy rolls.
  4. Rotate or discard unresolved Assignments as described below, exhaust non‑stored committed dice, perform Special Recovery, then normal recovery by REC. Set the dial to the Primary’s dot and advance.

After Evening, resolve End of Day.

The row is the same row all game: Assignments you do not clear stay on the table and follow you into the next Time Slot. A slot holds a maximum of 4 Assignments — if the row is already full when you reveal, discard both revealed cards and take nothing.

Special Recovery Recover all exhausted dice of the queued recovery color. Then recover dice equal to your REC separately.

STR in NAP

STR is the number of Strategy rolls available in the Time Slot, and it resets every slot. That is all it governs. It does not cap how many Assignments you may complete. One roll’s successes can be spread across several Assignments, and cards already holding stored progress can finish alongside them, so clearing more Assignments than you have Strategy rolls is normal — it is what storing progress is for.

Working Assignments

  • You may spread dice across as many Assignments as you like during a roll.
  • Required Effort. Every roll must be a real attempt: choose at least one Assignment — a Setback if one is present — and commit either one die matching that Assignment’s color dot, Red, or at least two dice of another color. Empty rolls are illegal. Waive this only if no legal commitment is possible at all.
  • To complete an Assignment or store progress on it, the successes you apply must include a successful die matching its color dot, Red as wild, or two successful dice of the same other color. The color rule governs storing as well as finishing — you cannot bank progress with dice that could never have counted.
  • You may spend 1 STR to remove the initial Assignment without gaining its benefit.

Unresolved Assignments

  • Successful dice on an unresolved Assignment may be stored as progress instead of exhausted.
  • At the end of the Time Slot, every unresolved Task or Setback flips to its Demand side — a harsher printed effect, and often a higher Target. Flipping costs nothing by itself.
  • An unresolved Opportunity rotates 180 degrees for one final chance in the next Time Slot. Opportunities store no progress. If the rotated Opportunity is still incomplete at the end of that slot, discard it without applying an effect or penalty.
  • An Assignment that is already on its Demand side and still unresolved at the end of a slot inflicts its printed Demand penalty, and does so again at the end of every slot it survives. When a penalty exhausts dice, exhaust dice of that Assignment’s recovery‑dot color first; if you hold none, exhaust your weakest.
  • Once the next Time Slot begins, stored dice are locked to that Assignment and cannot be voluntarily exhausted.
  • When an Assignment finally resolves, its committed and stored dice exhaust unless the card says otherwise. A Credit Task completed on its Demand side still earns its Credit.
  • Rotate every completed Task, Opportunity, or Setback 90 degrees clockwise and leave it in its Time Slot until End of Day. Resolve its completion reward before continuing. Completed cards no longer count toward the limit of 4 unresolved Assignments in that row.
  • Completing an Opportunity never pays out on the spot: you keep the card as a coupon (see Coupons).

Coupons

An Opportunity pays nothing when you complete it. Instead you keep the card in front of you as a coupon. A coupon has no expiry: spend it whenever you like, on any Day, including during the Final Exam. Each is one use — spend it, apply the effect, then discard the card.

OpportunitySpend it to…
Take an Unauthorized BreakCancel 1 Health loss.
Raid the Supply ClosetCount one die as any color for one roll.

Coupons that add successes still obey the color rule: they add to an application that already includes a legal die. You may hold any number of coupons, and unspent coupons score nothing at the end of the game.

Skulls Skulls resolve only on Assignments you chose. Ignore Skulls on cards added by another Skull, by FINF, or by a forced replacement. If a Skull would add an Assignment while the slot already holds 4, do not add a card — instead rotate the Skull‑generating Assignment to its Demand side once. Further blocked Skulls do nothing.

End of Day

Move all completed Assignments from the Time Slots to the face‑up discard pile. Reveal and discard the top card of the Assignment deck. Ignore all of its text, effects and Skulls.

The Final Exam

Leaving the apprenticeship

Discard unresolved Assignments and exhaust their stored dice. If you are short of your Credit goal, exhaust 1 available die per missing Credit in the order Red, Purple, Blue, Green, Yellow, looping as needed.

Preparation

  • Recover 1 exhausted die.
  • Recover 1 further exhausted die per Credit above your Credit goal. Excess‑Credit recovery may not pick the same color twice.
  • Perform the queued Morning recovery.

Shuffle the pool and draw 3 Final Exams.

Resolving the exams

  1. Reduce the exam’s base Target by 1 for each Credit you hold in that subject.
  2. Roll against the modified Target, honoring the exam’s printed restriction.
  3. Lose 1 Health for each success below the modified Target. If you still have Health, you passed.
  4. Perform Special Recovery using that exam card’s own color dot, then resolve the next exam.

All three exams are mandatory. FINF is not used during the Final Exam. Finish all three with at least 1 Health to win NAP.

Carrying NAP forward

Only relevant if you are playing the Nobulous Research Journal (NRJ) campaign.

The 3 Final Exams you actually faced are retained. Three unused Final Exams become candidates. NAP contributes 6 cards to the Research Pool.
