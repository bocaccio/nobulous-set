PRINT AND PLAY CONTENTS
Letter paper, 9 cards per page (3 x 3), poker size 63.5 x 88.9 mm.
Pages of landscape cards are themselves landscape - print at 100%,
scale to fit OFF, and let the printer auto-rotate.

Sections marked DUPLEX are genuinely two-sided: their pages alternate
front / back with the back columns mirrored. Print those sheets
double-sided, flip on the LONG edge.
