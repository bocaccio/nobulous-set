PRINT AND PLAY CONTENTS
Letter paper, 9 cards per page (3 x 3), poker size 63.5 x 88.9 mm.
Print at 100%, scale to fit OFF.

Pages alternate fronts and backs, labelled in the bottom margin:
Page 1 - Front, then Page 1 - Back for those same nine cards. The
back columns are mirrored, so print double-sided and flip on the
LONG edge.
