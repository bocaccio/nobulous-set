# Notice

The Nobulous Set is an unofficial fan expansion for **20 Strong**, published by
Chip Theory Games. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced in
association with Chip Theory Games.

It requires a copy of 20 Strong to play: the 17 unit dice, the three stat dice
and the Chip are used at the table and are not reproduced here.

No Chip Theory Games artwork, text or trademarks are reproduced in this
repository. All card layouts, card text, rules text and generator code here are
original work, shared for free, non-commercial fan use.

That includes the Tabletop Simulator mod, which has to show dice and a Chip of
its own. Its eight die faces and both Chip faces are drawn by `tts/make_dice.py`
and `tts/make_chip.py` — gears, arrows, numerals and the set's own Anatomy heart,
in the set's own palette and typeface. They are deliberately not copies: the
physical components carry a four-point sparkle inside a gear and a photographic
chip face, and neither appears here. The mod loads no asset from anywhere but
this repository, which `generator/check_app_sync.py` holds.

If you own 20 Strong and prefer the real components in Tabletop Simulator, use
the official mod's dice — nothing here depends on these faces beyond the angles
the Chip is turned to, which are the same either way.

If a rights holder would like anything here changed or removed, open an issue.
