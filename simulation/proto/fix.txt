WHAT THE TENSION COLUMN SAYS. Only the candidates that touch the
RECOVERY FLOOR move it. A, B, D, E and G leave `<=2` at 0% and
`empty` at 0.0% however far they move the win rate - G banishes
eight dice a game and changes the tension not at all. The reason
is that 'recover up to REC exhausted dice' refills the pool to a
fixed floor every attempt whatever happened, so the pool cannot
trend toward zero and running out cannot become a worry. Win rate
is reachable with any dial here; tension is reachable only by
making the floor itself fall.

RECOMMENDED: H. One line on the Imposter card -
  "Your REC is 3 minus the number of cards in the Suspicion row."
plus starting Health 5 rather than 4. No new components: the
Suspicion row is already face-up on the table, and it already
drives the Target climb, so the more the Council suspects you the
less you recover. It lands at the middle of the community band
with the highest dice tension in the set, and the pool declines
17 -> 3.3 -> 0.7 across the nine attempts instead of sitting on
a floor of 3. Partial payment on top is redundant and pushes it
to 82%.

The rungs are wider than the other three games', because nine
pass/fail attempts against a small failure budget amplify any
dial about ninefold. That steepness is the finale's real
structural problem and it is not fixed here - but it is what
makes one number serve as a four-rung ladder.
