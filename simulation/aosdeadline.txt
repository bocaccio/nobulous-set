  AND THE ONE WORD THAT DECIDES ALL OF IT: what "complete a
  Program" has to mean before it can be installed. The handoff
  says "after a clean run", and clean is AoS's own word for the
  Program Reward - no failed element at all. Charging the same
  failure twice, once for the Reward and once for the slot, is
  the whole difference between a broken game and a good one:
                                           Easy Standard     Hard Nobulous
no failed element (as written)             5.3%     2.7%     1.9%     1.2%
at most one failed element                47.9%    42.0%    35.7%    32.4%
just finishing the Program                55.6%    51.5%    50.4%    47.1%

  For reference, the AoS this replaced measured 60.6 / 52.0 /
  43.6 / 35.6 for a player who triages. "At most one failed
  element" lands just under it with the spread intact, and
  leaves all three failure modes live: at Standard, 29.9% lose
  to the deadline, 23.4% to Health, 4.8% in the finale.
