THE TIMING INSTEAD OF THE SIZE. The real complaint was not that
the refill is big, it is that it is CERTAIN: the Primary's dot
names the next slot's colour, so once purple is queued you
spend every purple you have. Making the recovery reach only
dice that were already exhausted when the slot began takes the
certainty away and leaves the size alone.

AND WHICH EXAMS YOU SIT - the half of the rule that is not free.
  one per subject, 3 of 4    a subject repeats   0.0% of games
                               Anat 75%  Engi 75%  Proc 75%  Theo 75%
  any 3 of the 12            a subject repeats  50.9% of games
                               Anat 62%  Engi 62%  Proc 62%  Theo 62%
  Drawing from all 12 repeats a subject in half of all games,
  and drops each subject's appearance from 75% to 62%. Credits
  reduce the Target of THAT subject's exam, so a repeat doubles
  the value of one specialism and strands the other three. To
  keep the shipped draw, select 1 per subject, shuffle the four
  face down, deal 3 to the Time Slots and box the fourth unseen
  - that is the current rule with a new table position, and it
  moves nothing.
