THE PROTOTYPE'S ERRATA - three finale10 claims that the real
components did not support:
  1 'the pace cannot be memorised'. The schedule rule IS the
    winning pace and it is one formula, so the depth is in
    execution. finale10 said as much in its honest-limit note;
    what is new is that its own throttled variant measures WORSE
    than the plain schedule here, 40.1% against 51.4%.
  2 'a three-colour window'. At three colours raw strength beats
    reading the wheel and the finale reads 79.2%. The window has
    to be TWO for the dial's central decision to pay at all.
  3 its pacing figures. `clock` rounds the schedule and so falls
    quietly behind at a pressure just over 1 - see how_many().
  4 'commit only the dice you need', its LARGEST lever at +35.1.
    Worth +11.8 here, a third of that - and it is the lever
    most sensitive to the recovery economy. At REC 4 with the
    five Reward cards paying dice back it INVERTS, and
    throwing every allowed die wins by 10.0, which is why
    REC is 3: the Rewards ARE the fourth point of recovery, and
    they have to be earned.
