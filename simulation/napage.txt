1. THE AGE RULE IS A KNOB. At the Standard rung, 5 Credits and
   2 Setbacks, six Apprentices averaged, 3000 games each.

   AN ALLOWANCE: '...plus up to N of the dice you spent this
   slot'. 4 is the whole of a colour, so it is the printed rule
   back, and that it lands on the shipped figure is the check
   that both ends are the same rule.

   Morning only                                45.9%    -3.0

   all but Morning                             33.1%   -15.8
   all but Evening                             35.2%   -13.7

   The allowance is the dial that behaves - it runs smoothly from
   the hard rule back to the printed one. Slot subsets are not a
   softener of the same kind: Morning is the recovery the Day has
   already paid for, so exempting it changes almost nothing while
   exempting the other two leaves the exploit exactly where Jon
   found it, at the end of the Afternoon.

2. WHAT PAYS FOR IT. The rule takes dice income away, so look at
   the other income first. REC and STR are 2 on all six
   Apprentice cards and so is HP. Same rung, same games, the hard
   age rule on throughout.

age rule, nothing paid back                    31.8%   -17.1
   ...and REC 3, a third die each End of Day   46.2%    -2.7
   ...and REC 4                                57.2%    +8.3
   ...and STR 3, a third Strategy roll         45.1%    -3.8
   ...and HP 3                                 42.5%    -6.4
   ...and REC 3 and STR 3                      61.8%   +12.9

   REC fits the hole the rule makes. +1 REC is worth about what
   the age rule costs, and it buys dice back WITHOUT buying the
   certainty back: an End of Day recovery is one choice of
   colours a day, not a colour you can see coming and dump into.
   STR 3 costs nearly as much and does hand the certainty back,
   because a spare Strategy roll is what the dumped dice are
   spent on; HP 3 pays at the exams, which is not where the
   decision was missing.

   THE REFILL COLUMN IS WHERE THIS DIFFERS FROM A CAP. A cap
   shrinks the refill and leaves the certainty exactly where it
   was, which is why capping at 2 read 4.4% and still answered
   nothing Jon raised. Some income does go with the age rule all
   the same - a die exhausted during a slot waits for its colour
   to come round again, and a colour that never comes round
   again never returns it - and that is what REC 3 pays back.
   The first four columns are the part he asked for: the pool in
   front of the player is smaller and tight far more often, so a
   die spent is a die missed.
